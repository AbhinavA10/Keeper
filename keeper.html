Every file in each group (e.g. NEF + JPG, HEIC + Live Photo MOV) and any NX Studio sidecars will be moved to the Recycle Bin.

Fix dates

Sets each JPEG's Date Modified to its Date Taken (from the photo's EXIF, or its NEF if the JPEG has none). Only the file's timestamp changes; the photo itself isn't touched.
